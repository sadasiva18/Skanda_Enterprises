Our Method

నూనె ఎలా తీస్తాము? Traditional Extraction Process

A transparent view into our chemical-free, slow extraction method.

01

Sourcing & Drying

We source rich seeds from local farms and sun-dry them until optimum moisture is reached. This naturally enhances shelf life.

02

Wooden Kolhu Press

Seeds are crushed in a wooden mortar (kolhu) using a slow pestle. No heat is generated during the extraction, preventing nutrient loss.

03

Sedimentation

Instead of refining with chemicals, the oil is kept in tanks for 3–4 days to let impurities settle down naturally.

04

Eco-Packaging

Pure oil is passed through double cotton cloth filters and packed in eco-friendly containers ready for your kitchen.
